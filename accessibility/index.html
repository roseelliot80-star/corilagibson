Accessibility

If something makes it difficult to read a guide, choose a plan, or retrieve a purchase, we welcome a description of the barrier.

Reading the site

Use your browser’s zoom controls to increase text size. For a saved PDF, use the zoom setting in your PDF reader or select the pages you want to print. Some readers may present printable layouts differently.

Tell us what got in the way

Use the Contact page. Include the title or address of the page, what you were trying to do, and the format you would find easier to use. If the issue happened in a specific browser, device, or assistive tool, you may include its name. You do not need to describe a disability.

Checkout and file access

If a payment or download control is difficult to use, ask for help before submitting payment again. We will review the issue and work with you on an accessible way to read the information or receive a purchased guide.
