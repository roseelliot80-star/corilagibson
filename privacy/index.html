Privacy and cookies

This policy explains how Corliss Gibson Yoga handles information when you visit corlissgibson.site, buy a digital guide, or contact us. Effective November 20, 2026.

Browsing the site

Cloudflare delivers these pages and files. In doing so, its infrastructure receives technical information such as the IP address, time, requested address, and browser information. We use the service to keep the site available and protect it against abuse.

Purchases and payment details

Stripe hosts checkout and processes payment information, checkout email, and information needed to complete or protect your transaction. Enter card details only on the hosted checkout. Our site checks payment status with Stripe and keeps an order reference, plan choice, creation time, Stripe session reference, and a hash of the browser access token for up to 30 days. Stripe has its own privacy policy and retention practices for payment records.

Cookies and download access

A necessary first-party cookie lets the browser that started your order retrieve the purchased PDF for up to 30 days. Clearing cookies or switching browsers can interrupt access. Save the file after payment. Stripe may use its own cookies or similar tools on its checkout for payment processing, security, and fraud prevention. Our guide pages do not load advertising pixels or third-party analytics.

When you contact us

The support form stores your email, message, ticket number, and submission time in Cloudflare’s database. We use these records to respond to your request and remove them on a scheduled basis after 90 days. Support replies sent by email may also be retained as needed to resolve your request, account for a refund, or meet legal obligations.

Security and sharing

To limit automated submissions, we retain a daily salted hash derived from a network address for up to 48 hours. We share information with service providers as needed to host the site, process payments, handle support, and protect orders. We may also disclose information when legally required. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choices

You can read the free guides without providing an email address. To ask for access, correction, or deletion, use the Contact page and identify the purchase or ticket concerned. We may verify your identity before responding and retain records needed for legal obligations or fraud prevention. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

Children and changes

The store is intended for adults. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. We will publish updates on this page with a revised effective date.
