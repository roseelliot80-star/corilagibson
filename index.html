Before you begin

Do I need previous yoga experience?

The guides start with seated basics. If you are new to yoga or unsure which movements suit you, ask a qualified instructor or healthcare professional for individual guidance. Read our safety guidance.

What will I receive?

A downloadable PDF in US Letter format. Read it on your device or print the pages you want. Each practice has written steps and space for a reflection.

Do I have to practice on consecutive days?

No. Each day is a page you can return to whenever you like. You can rest, skip a movement, or repeat a familiar page.

Can I get help with a purchase?

Use contact support for download problems, accessibility questions, or refund requests. See delivery and refunds for the 14-day policy.
