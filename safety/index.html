Practicing safely

These guides offer general education for self-directed practice. A printed page cannot check the way you are moving or determine which movements fit your health needs.

Before your first session

Discuss a new yoga practice with your healthcare professional, especially if you have balance concerns, a recent injury or surgery, or restrictions on movement. If you need help adapting a pose, a qualified instructor can observe you and suggest changes.

Set up your chair

Choose a firm, stable chair without wheels on a surface where it will not slide. Clear the space around your feet. Sit where your feet can rest securely, and adjust or omit any movement that would make you unsteady.

During practice

Move gently in a comfortable range and breathe normally. Avoid forcing a stretch, holding your breath, or working through pain. Take breaks whenever you want. Stop if you feel pain, dizzy, faint, or unusually short of breath.

If you feel unwell

Seek medical advice about new or persistent symptoms before resuming. In a medical emergency in the United States, call 911. Our support form is for store and guide questions.
