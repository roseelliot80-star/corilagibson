Delivery and refunds

Every paid guide is a downloadable PDF with a one-time USD price. You may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase, including after downloading the guide.

Download after checkout

Once Stripe confirms payment, return to the confirmation page in the same browser to download your guide. Save it to your device. The browser access cookie lasts up to 30 days. A saved PDF can be read or printed after that time. There is no physical shipment or recurring charge.

Request a refund

Send a message through the Contact page within 14 calendar days of purchase. Give us the email used at checkout, the plan name, and your purchase date or order reference. No reason is required. Do not include your payment card number.

How the money is returned

We verify the order and issue the full refund to the original payment method. Your bank determines when the credit appears. If you are waiting for a refund, contact us with the original ticket or order reference.

Missing files or duplicate charges

If payment succeeded but the file is unavailable, contact us before buying again. We can verify the order and help restore delivery. For a duplicate charge or a file that does not match the order, contact us even if the 14-day window has passed.

Cancellation and consumer rights

You can leave checkout before completing payment. For completed purchases, use the refund process above. Our voluntary refund policy is in addition to your rights under applicable law.
