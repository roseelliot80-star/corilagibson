Your first seated practice

Find your seat, follow one breath, and finish with a pause. This is the opening practice in all three workbooks.

Before you start
Place a firm, nonrolling chair on a level, nonslip surface. Both feet should reach the floor comfortably. Read the health and safety guide, especially if you have an injury or restrictions on movement.

1. Find your seat

Rest your hands on your thighs. Keep both feet comfortably apart and your eyes open. Let the chair carry your weight. If it feels comfortable, imagine a little space above the crown of your head.

2. Follow one breath

Notice one breath coming in and one breath going out. Let it happen at its usual pace. Soften your jaw if that feels comfortable. You can bring your attention back to the room at any point.

3. Rest in the chair

Let your back rest against the chair. Feel your feet on the floor and look at a familiar spot in the room. Stay for as little or as long as you like.

A note for next time

What chair and foot position felt most settled? Write a few words to remind yourself what you want to repeat. Stop if a movement hurts or you feel dizzy.
