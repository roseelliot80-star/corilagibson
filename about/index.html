About Corliss Gibson Yoga

Corliss Gibson Yoga publishes self-guided chair yoga reading plans for older adults in the United States.

A chair and a page

You can read the free guide online or choose a printable plan arranged over 7, 14, or 21 days. The day numbers give you a place to pick up again. Repeat a page, take a rest day, or skip a movement that does not suit you.

What you receive

Each paid plan is a written PDF in English for your own use. You can save it to a computer, tablet, or phone and print the pages you want. Each plan has a single purchase price in US dollars.

How the guides are made

Our materials are general educational reading. The photographs are AI-generated illustrations, and the people pictured do not provide instruction or testimonials. For advice about your own movement limits, work with a qualified professional who knows your needs.

Questions before buying

Use the Contact page to ask about a guide, a purchase, or an accessible way to read the material.
